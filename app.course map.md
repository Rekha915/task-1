## Semesters 7–8 (unconfirmed)
 
The published page lists Internship Program/Research 15 + Thesis & Seminar 15 in each semester, plus "as prescribed" professional/open electives, with no L-T-P figures. The page itself advises confirming this with the department, so these credits are **not used** in any calculation. Total with sems 7–8 as listed would be 193 plus electives.
 
## Elective alternatives (not summed)
 
Elective I (sem 5) and Elective II (sem 6) are 3 credits each. ICFAI lists AI & ML professional electives (3 credits, L=3, T=0, P=0) in five tracks: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Cyber-Physical Systems, Blockchain. These are mutually exclusive choices and are not added together.
 
| Track | Listed electives |
|---|---|
| Machine Learning | Machine Learning for IoT; Quantum Machine Learning Algorithms; Predictive Modelling in Healthcare; Medical Image Analysis; Clinical Decision Support Systems |
| Deep Learning | Neural Networks and Architectures; Introduction to GANs; Social Network Analysis and Recommendation Systems; Ethical Challenges in AI and Deep Learning; Deep Reinforcement Learning |
| Computer Vision | Image Formation and Representation; Object Recognition Algorithms; Depth Perception and Stereo Vision; Facial Expression Analysis; AR/VR Applications in Computer Vision |
| Cyber-Physical Systems | Fundamentals of CPS; Microcontroller Programming; Real-Time Computing; Smart Grids; Cyber-Physical Production Systems |
| Blockchain | Ethereum and Other Blockchain Platforms; Cryptographic Principles; Blockchain in Supply Chains; Data Privacy Regulations; Interoperability Protocols |
